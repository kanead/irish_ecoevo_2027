Plenary speakers

Plenary speaker

Owen Lewis

University of Oxford

Professor Owen Lewis is Professor of Ecology at the University of Oxford, specialising in community ecology, entomology and conservation biology. His research investigates the processes maintaining and threatening biodiversity, with particular emphasis on tropical rainforests, insect food webs, plant–herbivore interactions, and how land-use change and climate change affect biodiversity and ecosystem functioning..

Speaker profile
Plenary speaker

Catherine Farrell

Trinity College Dublin

Dr. Catherine Farrell is Assistant Professor of Business and Nature at Trinity Business School, Trinity College Dublin. A specialist in peatland restoration and nature conservation, her research explores natural capital accounting, nature-based solutions, and nature finance. She works with businesses, policymakers, and communities to develop credible strategies that support biodiversity and restoration.
